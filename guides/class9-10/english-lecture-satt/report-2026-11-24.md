Rules: identical → same; one side empty/stub → the other, BUT an old solution is preserved over an empty new one only if it positively matches — a writing-task answer must match type AND share a topic word, unless the evidence is insufficient (fragmentary solution < 5 topic words, e.g. 'Myself : Sir, may I come in?', or a topic-less question < 4 topic words, e.g. 'Write a paragraph describing the information of the following graph' — then the old answer stays on benefit of doubt); both real and differing → higher (type_ok, topic_hits) wins, tie → new; winner old → flag 'Solution kept from previous chunk; re-parse attached a mismatched solution.'
